# Portfolio Voice

Use this file when rewriting copy for this portfolio.

## Voice Profile

- Sound like a backend engineer who ships systems for real operations.
- Prefer operational language over aspirational language.
- Keep sentences direct, high-signal, and slightly hard-edged.
- Treat buzzwords as a smell unless they are attached to a concrete system or outcome.

## Current Positioning

- Backend engineer
- Focus on automation, integrations, queues, document workflows, OCR, and applied AI
- Emphasize reliability, observability, auditability, and fallback paths
- Speak to hiring managers looking for execution, not self-branding theater

## Rewrite Heuristics

- Replace generic benefits with the system, mechanism, and outcome.
- Name the workload: documents, queues, APIs, pipelines, legacy systems.
- Name the safeguard: retry, validation, telemetry, human review, fallback.
- Use metrics only when they are already stated or safely bounded.
- Preserve scanability on first read. A recruiter should understand fit in under 20 seconds.

## Patterns

Prefer:

- `I design backends, queues, and pipelines that connect data, OCR, LLMs, and legacy systems without sacrificing predictability.`
- `Queue-driven automation with observability, idempotency, and controlled fallback.`
- `Turn manual document handling into auditable workflows.`

Avoid:

- `I am passionate about solving problems with innovative technology.`
- `Results-driven engineer with a proven track record of excellence.`
- `Leveraging AI to create seamless end-to-end solutions.`
